No 3D File? No Problem

Three easy ways to order

Most people who order from us have never touched a 3D file. Here's how you can order anyway.

Option 1 · Easiest

Pick a design on MakerWorld

MakerWorld is a free website with thousands of ready-made designs — toys, decor, organisers, planters, figurines, gadgets and more.

  1. Open makerworld.com and search for anything — try "planter", "phone stand" or "dragon".
  2. Found something you like? Copy the page link.
  3. Send us the link on WhatsApp with the colour you want. We'll check it and send you a price.
Browse MakerWorld ↗
Option 2

Send a photo or sketch

Broken knob? A logo you want as a keychain? A rough drawing on paper? Take a photo and send it over.

  1. Snap a picture of the part, logo or sketch.
  2. Tell us roughly how big it should be.
  3. We'll tell you if it can be printed and what it costs. (Simple design work may have a small extra charge.)
Send a Photo on WhatsApp
Option 3

Just describe the idea

"A name plate for my daughter's room." "Return gifts for 15 kids." "Something for my dad's desk." That's enough to get started.

  1. Message us what it's for and your budget.
  2. We'll suggest a few options with prices.
  3. You pick one — we print it and message you when it's ready.

Material Comparison

Choose your material

Two reliable filaments, every colour in stock made available. Not sure which one fits your part? The table below breaks it down.

MaterialBest ForStrengthHeat ResistanceFinish
PLA Decorative items, miniatures, name plates, gifts, prototypes Rigid, but brittle under impact ~55–60°C Smooth, crisp detail, matte-to-glossy
PETG-HS Robotics parts, functional mounts, cosplay parts, outdoor use Tougher & more flexible, impact-resistant ~75–85°C Slight gloss, layer-strong, durable
TPU Flexible parts, phone cases, wearable bits Flexible, bendable, impact-absorbing ~60°C Soft-touch, semi-flexible

TPU is planned as a future material and is not open for orders yet. All colours are available, though a specific colour may occasionally be out of stock.

How It Works

Order process

01

Submit your request

Share your STL / G-code / STEP / OBJ / 3MF file, or just describe your idea via the order form or WhatsApp.

02

Get your quote

We check the file, confirm weight & print time, and send you a final price — use the calculator above for an instant estimate first.

03

Confirm & pay

Approve the quote and pay via Cash, UPI, Google Pay or any convenient method.

04

We print it

Printed on the Bambu Lab A1 Mini, then supports are fully removed and the part is quality-checked before handover.

05

Self pickup

We'll message you on WhatsApp when it's ready — collect it directly from us nearby. No delivery, keeps costs low for everyone.
