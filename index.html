Material Comparison

Choose your material

Two reliable filaments, every colour in stock made available. Not sure which one fits your part? The table below breaks it down.

MaterialBest ForStrengthHeat ResistanceFinish
PLA Decorative items, miniatures, name plates, gifts, prototypes Rigid, but brittle under impact ~55–60°C Smooth, crisp detail, matte-to-glossy
PETG-HS Robotics parts, functional mounts, cosplay parts, outdoor use Tougher & more flexible, impact-resistant ~75–85°C Slight gloss, layer-strong, durable
TPU Flexible parts, phone cases, wearable bits Flexible, bendable, impact-absorbing ~60°C Soft-touch, semi-flexible

TPU is planned as a future material and is not open for orders yet. All colours are available, though a specific colour may occasionally be out of stock.

How It Works

Order process

01

Submit your request

Share your STL / G-code / STEP / OBJ / 3MF file, or just describe your idea via the order form or WhatsApp.

02

Get your quote

We check the file, confirm weight & print time, and send you a final price — use the calculator above for an instant estimate first.

03

Confirm & pay

Approve the quote and pay via Cash, UPI, Google Pay or any convenient method.

04

We print it

Printed on the Bambu Lab A1 Mini, then supports are fully removed and the part is quality-checked before handover.

05

Self pickup

We'll message you on WhatsApp when it's ready — collect it directly from us nearby. No delivery, keeps costs low for everyone.
